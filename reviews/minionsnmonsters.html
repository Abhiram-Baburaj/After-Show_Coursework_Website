SUMMARY

Placing these famously chaotic characters into a tabletop RPG setting sounds like a bizarre gamble, but Illumination pulls it off with surprising charm. The fantasy tropes provide a structured framework for the physical comedy, resulting in a cohesive, fast-paced adventure that actually respects its D&D inspirations.
