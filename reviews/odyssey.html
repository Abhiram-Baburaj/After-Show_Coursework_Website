SUMMARY

A rare return to the massive, sweeping scale of classic historical epics. While the first act requires some patience as it establishes the sheer scope of the ancient world, the payoff is undeniable. The practical set design with barely unnoticable VFX and grueling survival sequences lead to a gritty realism to the mythology.
