SUMMARY

Correcting the pacing issues of the 2021 reboot, this sequel wisely cuts the exposition and gets straight to the actual tournament. The martial arts choreography is significantly sharper, the stakes feel immediate, and the signature fatalities are delivered with exactly the kind of brutal, practical-effect intensity the series demands.
