SUMMARY

A sharply directed tech thriller that examines the darker consequences of algorithm manipulation without ever feeling like a heavy-handed lecture. The pacing is relentless, tracking the escalation of data farming into a genuinely tense narrative that feels uncomfortably relevant to modern internet culture.
