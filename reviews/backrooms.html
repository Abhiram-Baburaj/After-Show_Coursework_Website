SUMMARY

Translating a viral internet creepypasta to the big screen is a massive risk, but the execution here is remarkably effective. Rather than relying on cheap jump scares, the film leans heavily into the creeping dread of liminal spaces, letting the endless, humming yellow hallways generate a genuine sense of claustrophobia and disorientation.
